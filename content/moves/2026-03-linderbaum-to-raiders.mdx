This entry is here to show the rubric disagreeing with the headline. Linderbaum
is the highest-paid interior lineman in the history of the sport, at $27 million
a year, and it still comes out a 2.

That's the lesson worth taking from it: **positional value is the thing
newcomers miss.** A record contract sounds enormous, and if you'd said "biggest
signing of the offseason" in a bar you'd have been gently corrected. The same
$81 million spent on a quarterback or an edge rusher would be a different
conversation entirely.

Extensions like this are how good teams keep their best defenders instead of losing them later. New England agreed terms with Christian Gonzalez on a four-year, $135 million deal that, per the report, makes him the highest-paid cornerback the league has ever had. No draft picks move; the club is simply paying a premium to remove any doubt about who covers the opposing team's best receiver.

For a Bills supporter this matters because New England is a division rival, and Buffalo will play them twice every season. A settled, well-paid top cornerback in that defence is one more obstacle in the games that decide who wins the AFC East, and it signals a rival that intends to spend to compete rather than rebuild quietly.

Donald walked away from the game and has now formally walked back into it, signing for a single season in Los Angeles. Because he was not under contract to anyone, the Rams did not have to trade anything to get him — they simply outbid the field, and the short term means the club carries no long-term burden if the comeback does not work.

Buffalo fans have no divisional stake here, but this is the kind of move that quietly changes the shape of a season. A contender adding a defender of that standing raises the bar in the other conference, and if Buffalo reach the final game they may have to solve him.

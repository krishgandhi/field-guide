Miami's general manager and head coach both came from Green Bay, where they
watched Willis every day for two seasons. That's the whole logic of the signing:
they think they know something about him the rest of the league doesn't.

The reason to care as a Bills fan is that Miami are in Buffalo's division, so
this is a team the Bills play twice. And the $99 million is not a typo — it's
one of the largest sums any team has ever agreed to pay a player to leave.

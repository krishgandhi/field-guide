Dallas has agreed terms with veteran outside linebacker Von Miller on a one-year contract, according to a source cited by ESPN. Because he was unsigned, no picks or players go the other way — the club is adding a pass rusher for a single season and can walk away at the end of it. Deals like this arrive in the middle of August, once teams have watched their own players in camp and decided where they are still thin.

For a Buffalo fan there is nothing here that changes the Bills' roster or their schedule. It is worth filing away only as an example of how a short veteran contract works: no trade cost, no long-term money, and a club buying one year of a familiar name to help get after opposing quarterbacks.

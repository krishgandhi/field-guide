The one on this list that should matter most to you personally. New England are
in Buffalo's division, which means the Bills play them twice every season, and
they just made their offence meaningfully harder to defend.

The June timing is the part worth understanding, because it comes up constantly
and nobody explains it: teams wait until after June 1 to make expensive trades
so the accounting hit lands across two seasons instead of one.

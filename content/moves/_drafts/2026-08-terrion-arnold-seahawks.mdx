Arnold reaches free agency with charges pending and still draws interest from Houston, New Orleans and the Giants before picking Seattle. A one-year agreement is the standard shape for this situation: the team gets a look at a player it might not otherwise have access to, and it can walk away in a few months if the football or the legal side goes badly.

For a Bills supporter this barely registers. Seattle sits in the other conference, and the only broader point is a reminder that useful defenders keep appearing on the market late in the summer, so a roster hole in August is not necessarily a hole in September.

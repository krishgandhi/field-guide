McDermott was fired on 19 January, after an overtime loss to Denver in the
divisional round. Brady had the job eight days later on a five-year deal.

Promoting the offensive coordinator rather than hiring from outside is a
specific choice: it says the roster is fine and the problem was the ceiling,
not the foundation. Teams that think they need rebuilding hire strangers.

Worth knowing in conversation, because it's the thing every Bills fan has an
opinion about and nobody will explain to you first.

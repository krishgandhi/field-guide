New England is expected to bring in defensive tackle DaQuan Jones on a one-year contract worth as much as $6 million. That is the kind of deal clubs use to fill a specific hole quickly: a known veteran, one season of commitment, and no long-term money tied up if it does not work out. Interior linemen like Jones mainly exist to clog the middle so that the faster players around them can chase the ball.

For a Bills fan the sting is not the player so much as the address. Jones had been on Buffalo's roster, and he is now heading to a team Buffalo plays twice a season in the AFC East. It is a small move in isolation, but division rivals getting a useful, cheap starter is the sort of thing that quietly shows up in January.

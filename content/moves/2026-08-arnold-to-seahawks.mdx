Arnold is facing eight felony charges in Florida, relating to an incident in
February. He has pleaded not guilty and has denied the allegations through his
representatives, and the case has not been to trial.

That is the reason a first-round cornerback is available on a one-year minimum
deal, and it is why he visited the Giants, Saints and Texans first. The football
read is simple: at that price a team risks nothing, and the contract is short
enough to end quietly if either the football or the legal situation goes badly.

Very little of this touches Buffalo — Seattle are in the other conference. The
useful general point is that August is when the leftovers get signed, so a hole
in a roster now is not necessarily a hole in September.

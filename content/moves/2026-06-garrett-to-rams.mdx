This is the one to measure every other move against. It scores near the ceiling
on all six axes at once, which almost nothing does — most trades are big on two
or three and ordinary on the rest.

The short version: Los Angeles decided the next two seasons are worth more than
the next five drafts, and Cleveland agreed to be the team that stockpiles those
drafts. If you only remember one thing, remember that a trade is two different
bets, and only one of them can look smart in three years.

Read this one next to the Garrett trade, because it's the same team doing the
same thing twice. The Rams have now spent two future drafts inside one offseason
buying players who help immediately — that is a front office that has decided
its window is open and is not planning past it.

The Kansas City half is the more interesting conversation. Trading a 25-year-old
All-Pro is what a rebuilding team does, and Kansas City are not supposed to be
rebuilding.

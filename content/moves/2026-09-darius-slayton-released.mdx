A release is the bluntest transaction in the sport: the club ends the contract, gets nothing back, and the player can join anyone who wants him. Darius Slayton had been in New York longer than any other player on the roster, and he is now available on the open market ahead of the season.

The direct effect on Buffalo is small. The only real angle is that an experienced receiver is suddenly free to sign anywhere, and teams short of bodies at the position — including contenders looking for depth — now have an option that costs them no draft picks.

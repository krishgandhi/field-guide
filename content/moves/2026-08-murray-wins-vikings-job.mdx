This is the entry to read if you want to see why the checklist beats a
headline. Nothing was traded and almost no money changed hands, so three of the
six scores are ones — and it is still a big deal, entirely because of the
position and what the decision admits.

The thing to say out loud: a team does not bench a first-round quarterback
unless it has stopped believing in him, or has decided it cannot afford to wait.
Minnesota have not said which, and that is the conversation.

Josh Allen is the whole offence — he throws it and he runs it, and the team
goes as far as he carries them. New voice in charge this year after Sean
McDermott was let go, and the books are tight enough that roster moves are
forced rather than chosen: Buffalo carry a lot of dead money against the
salary cap.

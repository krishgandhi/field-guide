Four rushers is standard. Send five or six and you are blitzing — more pressure,
faster, but fewer defenders left to cover receivers.

It is a bet. Get there in time and the quarterback is on the ground; miss and
someone is running free downfield. When a commentator says a defense "got
burned," a blitz is often what burned it.

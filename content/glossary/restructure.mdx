The player is paid the same money, just relabelled. Base salary counts fully this
year; a signing bonus spreads across the deal. Swap one for the other and this
season's number drops while future seasons rise.

Josh Allen restructured in 2026, cutting his cap hit to $44.2 million and freeing
about $12 million right away. It is borrowing, and the bill arrives later.

A bonus can be spread across up to five contract years, so teams bolt on years
everyone knows the player will never play. More years, smaller yearly slices,
more room today.

When those years void, the remaining slices land at once as dead money. Matt
Milano's void year alone costs the Bills over $11 million against the 2026
salary cap, for a player not on the roster.

The player gets the cash immediately. The team does not take the salary cap
charge immediately — it splits the bonus into equal slices, one per contract
year, up to five. A $50 million bonus over five years counts $10 million a year.

This one rule explains most cap news you will ever hear. Every trick teams use is
some way of moving those slices around.

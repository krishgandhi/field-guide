Every team gets the same spending limit, and it is genuinely hard — no going over,
no paying a tax to exceed it. For 2026 it is $301.2 million per club, the first
time it has cleared $300 million.

This is why a team can want a player, afford him in cash, and still not sign him.
Almost every confusing roster move traces back to this number.

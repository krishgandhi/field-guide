One player per team, one year, fully guaranteed. The salary is set by formula:
the average of the top five at his position over the past five years, or 120% of
his last salary, whichever is higher.

It buys a team time when they cannot agree on a long-term deal. Players tend to
hate it — a year of risk with no security beyond it.

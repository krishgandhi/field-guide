Defenders have to respect the run. Fake it convincingly and linebackers step
toward the line — which is exactly where they should not be if the ball is going
over their heads.

Watch Josh Allen's back foot: if he turns and extends the ball to a running back
who does not have it, the throw behind that is the whole point.

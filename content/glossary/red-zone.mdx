Close to the goal line there is no deep field left, so defenders compress into a
smaller box and throwing windows shrink. Offenses that move easily between the
twenties often stall here.

"Red zone efficiency" is just how often a team turns these trips into
touchdowns instead of field goals. It decides a lot of close games.

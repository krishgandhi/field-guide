Picks are money. A first-rounder is worth far more than a second, a second far
more than a third, and the drop is steep rather than gradual.

This is why "they gave up a first and a third" is a real price and not trivia.
Spend enough of it on one player and you have no cheap young talent coming in
behind him for years.

When a deal is reported as "four years, $120 million," most of that is not
promised. Teams can cut a player and stop paying the rest. The guaranteed portion
is what he keeps regardless.

So when someone says a contract is smaller than it looks, this is what they mean.
Ask what is guaranteed, not what the total says.

Dead money is cap space a team spends on a player who is no longer on the
roster. A signing bonus is paid up front but charged against the cap in yearly
slices. Cut or trade the player early and every remaining slice lands at once.

So if the Bills release someone with two years of bonus left, that charge stays
on their books — money spent on a player now lining up somewhere else.

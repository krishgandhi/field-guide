Cut or trade a player early and every unused slice of his signing bonus lands on
the salary cap at once. He is gone; the charge is not.

The Bills carry about $45.8 million of it into 2026 — real money, already spent,
buying nothing. When a team looks strangely poor for its record, this is usually
why.

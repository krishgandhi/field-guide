The opposite of zone coverage: you get a person, not a patch of field, and you
stay with him all play.

Tighter, but it is one-on-one — beat your guy and nobody is behind you to help.
Easy to spot on TV, because a defender turning and sprinting downfield alongside
a receiver is not guarding an area.

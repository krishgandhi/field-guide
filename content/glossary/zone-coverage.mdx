Each defender owns a patch of grass and covers whoever enters it. Nobody chases
anybody across the field.

It keeps everything in front of you and makes long plays harder, at the cost of
gaps between the patches. When you hear a receiver "found the soft spot," he sat
down in the seam between two zones.

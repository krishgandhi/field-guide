Yellow
Line

The yellow first-down line is the one piece of broadcast graphics built to explain football to people watching it. This direction makes that line the product's mark: every explained term sits on it, every "watch this" signal is its colour. Around it, a social-sports app on a night-turf ground: chunky 22px cards, jersey-numeral ratings, condensed down-and-distance labels, a bottom tab bar. The field shows up only where a game is the subject. Krish's own flags are the red challenge flag: he is contesting the call, and the queue is where the review happens.

Components and conflicts

Components

  • Explained term: 3px first-down-yellow rule under the word; on tap it floods to a full yellow block while the sheet rises. No other element on the site uses yellow under text.
  • Jersey rating tile: 48×52 numeral, 5 filled yellow, 4 yellow outline, 3 chalk outline, 1–2 quiet. Always paired with a two-word label and a plain sentence.
  • Field card: mown-stripe turf with yard lines, logos in the end zones. Reserved for the one game that matters to him this week.
  • Yard bar: rubric axis score as five turf segments with hash ticks.
  • Trade ledger: two-column "gets" card; picks as numeral chips.
  • Tab bar: five tabs, active one gets a pill and a yellow line along the top edge.

Where this conflicts with CLAUDE.md §10

  • Concept: replaces "Apple Sports / Real" with a Sleeper-energy social sports app plus football material.
  • Type: "one family, Inter" becomes two: Big Shoulders Display for headings and numerals, Archivo (incl. its condensed width) for UI and reading.
  • Palette: amber #FFC53D becomes first-down yellow #F5E12A; pencil #FF6B5E becomes challenge red #FF5B4F; royal #4C8DFF becomes #5B95FF; a new mint #3DE3A6 means "checked". Ink, surfaces and line keep their roles at slightly greener values.
  • Signature: the dotted amber underline becomes a solid 3px yellow rule. The reasoning in §10 still holds.

Content notes

  • All teams, records, injuries, kickoffs, scores (1–5) and reasons come from slate.json, standings.json and watchability.ts run against them. Moves, glossary and team prose are quoted from content/.
  • Proposed copy that doesn't exist yet: the rating labels for 2–4 ("Good watch", "If it's on"), "Who to know", "Rams get / Browns get", "Still lost? Flag it".
  • The three queued flags are illustrative. The first is a real problem: division games don't count double, so the template sentence should be rewritten.
